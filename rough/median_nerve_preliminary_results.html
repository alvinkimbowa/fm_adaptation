Median nerve segmentation: Preliminary results

Test Dice (%) per scan location, 59 test images from 3 subjects not seen in training. One training run per model.

Data

Median nerve: labelled images per location

  • Train 193, validation 38, test 59 images. Test subjects (A, K, W) are not in training.
  • Distal forearm has the least: 10 training images, all from 1 clip of 1 subject.
  • Two probes: most clips use SL2325 (preset "PN"). SL1543 (preset "UPPER ARM") is 27 of 193 training images (2 clips) and 12 of 59 test images: both of subject A's test clips, i.e. all 5 distal-forearm and 7 of the 22 mid-forearm test images.

Other structures: labelled frames

  • 340 frames in the all-structures set; 290 have the median nerve, 50 have only other structures.
  • Only the brachial artery and humerus have more than 30 frames. Most structures come from 1–5 clips.

Question 1

Baseline: one labelled frame, median nerve only

Median Dice 84–92% at wrist, elbow and mid-arm, 41% at mid-forearm, 0% at distal forearm.

  • When it fails, it does not find the nerve at all: 8 of 22 mid-forearm images and all 5 distal-forearm images score close to 0%.
  • Most misses are on the SL1543 probe: 10 of its 12 test images are missed, against 3 of the 47 SL2325 test images.
  • On SL2325 alone, mid-forearm median Dice is 66%.

Question 2

Does knowing the location, or labelling surrounding tissue, help?

No. Neither raises mid-forearm or distal forearm.

  • Location given to the model: results about the same as without it.
  • Surrounding tissue labelled (22 structures): mean mid-forearm Dice drops from 34% to 26%.
  • Only 97 of 236 training frames had any structure other than the nerve labelled, mostly the brachial artery.

Question 3

Does video context help, with only the target frame labelled?

A little, at mid-forearm only. Not yet confirmed.

  • Mid-forearm mean Dice goes from 34% to 47%; images where the nerve is not found go from 7 to 4 of 22.
  • Other locations are about the same.
  • Distal forearm is still 0%.
  • To confirm, the model must also be trained with copies of the labelled frame in place of its neighbours. If that scores the same, the gain is not from the video. This check has not been run yet.

Way forward

More labels are needed for the following locations, in decreasing order of priority

  1. Distal forearm
  2. Mid-forearm
  3. Mid-arm
  4. Elbow
  • Label new clips from new subjects. More frames from clips we already have are less useful, since neighbouring frames look alike.
